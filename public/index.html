WeWrite

Write a handful of characters. A style-conditioned LSTM writes the rest of A-Z, a-z, 0-9 and common punctuation in your hand, and the server assembles an installable TrueType font.

1 Write these characters

Use a mouse, touchpad, pen or finger. Keep lowercase letters between the two guide lines, like on ruled paper. You can change the character above any box.

Or upload a photo of your handwriting

Dark ink on light paper, one line, clear gaps between characters. Type the characters in the photo from left to right. Photos carry no pen timing, so stroke order is guessed; drawing gives a better style match.
